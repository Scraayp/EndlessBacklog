# EndlessBacklog

Welcome to the EndlessBacklog wiki — a free, self-hosted Trello alternative
and agile planning board.

EndlessBacklog gives you workspaces, boards, lists, and cards with real-time
drag-and-drop, labels, checklists, due dates, comments, attachments,
notifications, and role-based access — all on infrastructure you control, with
no per-board or per-seat limits.
