# Getting Started

## Creating an account

1. Go to your EndlessBacklog instance's URL and click **Sign up**.
2. Enter your name, email, and a password (at least 10 characters, with a
   letter and a number).
3. Check your inbox for a verification email and click the link. You can log
   in before verifying, but verifying confirms the address notifications and
   invites will use.

If your instance has OAuth login configured (Google, GitHub, or Microsoft),
you'll see those as one-click alternatives on the login/signup page.

## Logging in

Enter your email and password. If your account has two-factor authentication
enabled, you'll be asked for a 6-digit code from your authenticator app after
your password is accepted.

Forgot your password? Use **Forgot password?** on the login page — you'll get
a reset link by email, valid for one hour.

## Creating your first workspace

Everything in EndlessBacklog lives inside a **workspace** — a container for
boards and the people who can see them.

1. From **Your workspaces**, click **New workspace**.
2. Give it a name and a URL slug (auto-filled from the name).
3. You're automatically its first admin.

See [Workspaces](Workspaces.md) for roles and inviting teammates.

## Creating your first board

1. Inside a workspace, click **New board**.
2. Pick a name and a background color.
3. Your board starts with three lists — **To Do**, **In Progress**, **Done**
   — a common starting template. Rename, delete, or add more lists freely.

## Adding cards

Click **Add a card** at the bottom of any list, type a title, and press
Enter (or click **Add card**) — it keeps the composer open so you can add
several in a row; press Escape or click away to close it.

Click a card to open its detail view: add a description, due date, labels,
members, checklists, attachments, and comments. See
[Cards and Checklists](Cards-and-Checklists.md).

## Moving cards and lists

Drag a card to reorder it within a list or move it to another list — the
move is instant for you and syncs to everyone else viewing the board within
moments, over a live WebSocket connection (no refresh needed). Drag a list's
header to reorder lists the same way.
